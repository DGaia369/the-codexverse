# Pathway Two™ Naming Source

**Status:** Source  
**Source Period:** June 27 to June 29, 2026  
**Authority:** Original pathway excavation conversations

## Purpose

This document preserves the original emergence of the Naming™ during the architectural separation of Pathway Two™ and Pathway Three™.

Source material is not automatically canon.

## Original Architecture

Pathway One™ produced the First Inheritance™:

the Declaration™  
I Choose Me.

Pathway Two™ was then proposed as:

The Role That Stayed Alive

Its central question was:

Who kept answering for me?

Pathway Three™ became:

the Agreement™

Its central question was:

What gave that role authority, and what do I authorize now instead?

## Original Naming™ Proposal

The Naming™ was proposed as a working name for the Second Inheritance™.

Its recognition was:

I now know which role kept answering for me.

The proposed artifact was a Role Record rather than a covenant.

The participant would name:

- the role
- what it protected
- what it cost
- where it continued answering

The Naming™ did not author a new agreement.

It rendered recognition as evidence.

## Other Names Considered

- Who Answered For Me™
- the Role That Answered™
- the Role I Carried™
- the Role Record™
- the Survival Role™

Who Answered For Me™ later became the preferred alternative during that excavation.

## Source Status

The Naming™ was proposed but never constitutionally locked as the Second Inheritance™.

This source is preserved for future Pathway Two™ architecture and Recognition Archaeology™.
