# Recognition Record™

**Status:** Canon  
**Locked:** July 25, 2026

## Definition

A Recognition Record™ is the canonical participant artifact of the codeXverse™.

It preserves the participant’s own evidence of what became visible, what she named, what she claimed, and what she is no longer willing to abandon.

A Recognition Record™ is not a report, score, performance record, or interpretation imposed on the participant.

## Relationship

**Recognition Record™**  
*the artifact*

**Part of the Library of Yourself™**  
*the collection*

## Canon rule

Recognition Record™ names what is created.

the Library of Yourself™ names where it belongs.

These terms are complementary and must not replace one another.

## Current implementation

The completed Declaration™ PDF generated through Pathway One™ is a Recognition Record™.

Future pathways may create additional Recognition Records™.
