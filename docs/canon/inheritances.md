# Inheritances™

**Status:** Active canon with unresolved entries

## Definition

An Inheritance™ is what becomes available to the participant when recognition has been claimed and embodied sufficiently to become hers.

An inheritance is not a reward for performance.

It is not a badge, score, credential, or proof of superiority.

## First Inheritance™

**Name:** I Choose Me: the Declaration™  
**Pathway:** Pathway One™ — Return to Self  
**Status:** Locked and implemented

### Function

The participant authors and seals her Declaration™.

The completed Declaration™ becomes a Recognition Record™ and is part of the Library of Yourself™.
