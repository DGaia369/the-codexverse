# Participant Lifecycle

**Status:** Current-state outline

## 1. Arrival

The participant arrives at the Threshold™.

The arrival stance is not:

> Who am I?

It is:

> Is there still a me underneath all of this?

## 2. Recognition

The participant encounters the Telling™, Recognition Fragments™, and the fixed Witness Sequence.

The ecosystem does not explain her to herself.

It creates conditions in which she recognizes what is already true.

## 3. Consent

Consent is explicit.

The current entry mechanism uses email only.

## 4. Pathway participation

The participant enters a Pathway™ and moves through its recognition sequence.

## 5. Integration

The participant names what became visible and begins acting from it.

Integration is not measured as worthiness or ranked progress.

## 6. Inheritance

The participant receives or authors the Inheritance™ associated with the pathway.

## 7. Recognition Record™

The participant’s artifact is created.

## 8. the Library of Yourself™

The Recognition Record™ becomes part of the participant-facing collection.

## 9. Return

The participant may return to her Library, revisit prior evidence, and enter later pathways.

## Current implementation boundary

The full multi-record Library experience is not yet implemented.

The architecture must preserve the distinction between artifact and collection when it is created.
