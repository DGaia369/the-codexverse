## Pathway Two™

**Name:** ReMEMBER  
**Status:** Name and direction locked. Full architecture still in development.

### Core movement

She re-members the scattered pieces of herself, including the roles she assumed to make others comfortable, remain accepted, or avoid abandonment.

### Core outcome

She becomes whole in the knowledge that her value and worthiness are non-negotiable.

Accepting crumbs of affection or attention is no longer available to her.

Shrinking to make others feel important is no longer available to her.

### Central practice

Presence without self-abandonment.

She notices where access has been granted at her own expense and begins withdrawing that access.
