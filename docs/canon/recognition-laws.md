# Recognition Laws™

**Status:** Canon

## Foundational laws

### Recognition before transformation

Recognition is the mechanism.

Transformation is a possible consequence.

### Technology helps humans see themselves clearly

Technology serves recognition. It does not replace human authorship.

### Once you see it, you cannot unsee it

Recognition changes what can continue unnoticed.

### Agreement is authorship

A true agreement is participant-authored.

### Artifact becomes evidence

What the participant creates becomes part of the evidence she can return to.

### Every subsequent pathway expands possibility, not worthiness

No pathway increases the participant’s worth.

### Recognition is the only currency the codeXverse™ trades in

The ecosystem does not trade in dependency, performance, compliance, or emotional capture.

### The value of a message is released by the conditions under which the message arrives

Timing, consent, sequence, and readiness matter.

## Threshold law

the Telling™ appears when adaptation obscures something that should have remained visible.

## Fragment law

A Recognition Fragment™ does not create the whisper.

It finds the whisper already alive.

## Library law

A Recognition Record™ is the artifact.

the Library of Yourself™ is the collection that houses those artifacts.
