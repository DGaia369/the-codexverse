# the Library of Yourself™

**Status:** Canon  
**Locked:** July 25, 2026

## Definition

the Library of Yourself™ is the participant-facing collection that houses her Recognition Records™.

It is where the participant returns to revisit, reconnect with, and continue gathering the evidence of her own life.

## Purpose

The Library of Yourself™ makes recognition accessible across time.

It does not replace the Recognition Record™. It contains Recognition Records™.

## Participant-facing relationship

A Recognition Record™ becomes:

> Part of the Library of Yourself™

## Current language

> Return to your library.  
> It already remembers.

## Future direction

Each inheritance, declaration, agreement, or other pathway artifact may become a Recognition Record™ and appear in the Library of Yourself™.

The Library should eventually support continuity across pathways without turning recognition into progress scoring.
