# Canon Terminology

**Status:** Living canon

## the codeXverse™

A Recognition Ecosystem™.

Always style with lowercase “the”:

> the codeXverse™

## Recognition Ecosystem™

A behavioral mirror environment and return-to-self architecture.

Its purpose is not to change participants.

Its purpose is to create conditions under which participants recognize themselves.

Transformation may follow. Recognition is the mechanism.

## Recognition Record™

The canonical participant artifact.

## the Library of Yourself™

The participant-facing collection that houses Recognition Records™.

## Recognition before transformation.

A core positioning statement and operating principle.

## the Telling™

Communication before language, registered by the nervous system.

Always lowercase “the.”

## Flutter™

An almost imperceptible signal.

## Visceral™

An embodied signal that is harder to dismiss.

## the Cracking™

The first visible evidence that containment is failing.

## Ghost Register™

Physical presence with experiential absence.

## Recognition Fragment™

A short phrase that finds recognition already alive in the participant.

The fragment does not create the whisper.

## Threshold™

The conditions that make visible what was normalized.

## Recognition Archaeology™

The discipline.

## Recognition Architecture™

The organized body of discoveries produced by Recognition Archaeology™.

## Inheritance™

What becomes available to the participant through recognized and claimed authorship.

## Pathway™

A structured recognition environment within the codeXverse™.

## Canon style rules

- No em dashes in participant copy.
- No AI patterning.
- No ladder stacking.
- Do not describe the codeXverse™ as an AI companion, therapy substitute, funnel, or optimization program.
- Prefer created, emerged, came into form, or came through over built when speaking about the ecosystem.
