# Pathway Two™ Naming Evolution

**Status:** Excavation  
**Pathway:** Pathway Two™  
**Current Pathway Name:** ReMEMBER™

## Excavation Question

What role does the Naming™ hold now that Pathway Two™ has evolved into ReMEMBER™?

## Earlier Function

When Pathway Two™ was The Role That Stayed Alive, the Naming™ was proposed as the Second Inheritance™.

The participant would recognize:

Who kept answering for me?

The inheritance would preserve the role, what it protected, what it cost, and where it still operated.

## Architectural Evolution

Pathway Two™ later became ReMEMBER™.

Its outcome expanded from identifying a survival role to recovering the scattered pieces of self and becoming whole.

The pathway’s center moved from pattern recognition to integration.

Earlier movement:

Recognition  
Name the role  
Receive the Naming™

Current movement:

Recognition  
Recover the scattered self  
Become whole

## Current DD Read

The Naming™ remains valuable, but it is not currently large enough to represent the full outcome of ReMEMBER™.

It may belong inside the pathway as a recognition movement.

It should not presently be treated as the Second Inheritance™.

## Possible Function Within ReMEMBER™

The Naming™ may help the participant recognize:

- the roles she took on
- what each role protected
- what each role required her to silence
- where each role still answers
- which pieces of herself were left behind

Naming would then support recovery.

It would not be the final inheritance.

## Preserved Questions

- Does the Naming™ remain a named movement?
- Does “Who answered for me?” belong as a central prompt?
- Does the role record become part of the final Recognition Record™?
- Does Who Answered For Me™ replace the Naming™?
- Is the role architecture one movement within a larger ReMEMBER™ sequence?

## Current Disposition

Remain Excavation.

Do not promote the Naming™ to canon.

Do not define it as the Second Inheritance™ without a new constitutional ruling.
