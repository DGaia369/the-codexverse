# Pathway Two™ Naming Evolution

**Date Recorded:** July 27, 2026  
**Authority:** Diana Francis  
**Status:** Historical Record

## Earlier State

During the June 2026 excavation, Pathway Two™ was proposed as The Role That Stayed Alive.

the Naming™ was proposed as a working name for the Second Inheritance™.

Its recognition was:

I now know which role kept answering for me.

Who Answered For Me™ was later considered a stronger alternative.

Neither name was constitutionally locked.

## Subsequent Evolution

Pathway Two™ later became ReMEMBER™.

Its core outcome expanded from identifying the role that continued answering to recovering the participant’s scattered pieces of self and becoming whole.

the Agreement™ moved to Pathway Three™ and became the Third Inheritance™.

## Current State

Pathway Two™ is ReMEMBER™.

The Second Inheritance™ remains intentionally undefined.

the Naming™ is preserved as an earlier inheritance proposal and possible recognition movement within ReMEMBER™.

It is not current canon.
