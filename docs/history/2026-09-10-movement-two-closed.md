## Founder ruling

Diana completed the final end-to-end walkthrough of Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™, Movement Two: Separate the Self from the Role, and approved it in full: the six writing questions, three Witness Moments, Differentiation Recognition Mirror™, Bedrock ("What do you know now?"), Bedrock Witness, Recognition Lens, Closing, and Return flow.

**Status:** IMPLEMENTATION PASSED / FOUNDER-LOCKED / CLOSED.

The movement passed the four Founder Close Gate tests:

1. The sequence pulls inward rather than requiring cognitive decoding.
2. Witnesses allow recognition to accumulate without interrupting it.
3. The Mirror increases visibility without interpreting participant meaning.
4. Bedrock, Lens, and Closing leave the participant with more authority over herself, not less.

Movement Two may be reopened only for a genuine implementation defect, broken continuity, accessibility failure, constitutional conflict, or a further Founder-directed amendment. It must not be reopened because alternate wording or another design idea appears later.

The governing specification is `docs/design-specifications/pathway-two-remember-movement-two-v1.0-founder-locked-2026-09-09.md` (amended September 10, 2026 for the Bedrock question; see that document's own Bedrock Amendment section for the superseded wording, preserved inline).

## Governing laws reaffirmed by the walkthrough

Recognition Key Law: the question is the key, not the explanation. Founder Resonance Test: does the body begin answering before the mind starts explaining? Witness Law: a witness returns participant evidence without assigning meaning to it. Mirror Law: the Mirror increases visibility without increasing interpretation. Participant Authority Law: the participant remains the authority on what her own words mean. ME Moment Scaffolding Principle: the sequence may create conditions in which recognition surfaces organically; the codeXverse™ must never manufacture, announce, certify, or interpret a ME Moment for the participant.

## Second Inheritance™ — unchanged

The Second Inheritance™ remains intentionally undefined, per `docs/canon/inheritances.md`. The non-governing excavation signal recorded in `docs/excavations/pathway-two-naming-evolution.md` (self-witnessing, capacity, internal authority, ownership of recognition, interpretive sovereignty, awareness, freedom) is preserved unchanged and remains excavation only, not Canon. Movement Three: Recover What Was Suppressed remains closed for implementation. No participant copy, screens, database fields, migrations, routes, Bedrock, Recognition Lens, or transition language were created for it in this closure pass. A future Founder architecture session opens it separately.

## Library of Yourself™ continuity — recorded as a next bounded architecture item, not implemented

The Library of Yourself™ currently presents Pathway One™ (Return to Self™, Pathway One evidence, First Inheritance™, Between Thresholds). Pathway Two™ returns the participant to this Library after Movement Two completion, but there is no Pathway Two continuity presence there yet. This is explicitly **not** classified as a Movement Two defect; it is a separate Library continuity architecture decision, recorded here as an open question pending a Founder architecture ruling:

> How should the Library of Yourself™ acknowledge and preserve participant-owned evidence from Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™ without prematurely defining the Second Inheritance™?

Until that ruling, no Pathway Two card, artifact, or Recognition Record™ is added to the Library, no evidence classification is defined, no Second Inheritance™ is inferred, and `/record` is not redesigned.
