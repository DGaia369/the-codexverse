# Artifact and Library Evolution

**Status:** Historical excavation

Recognition Record™ is the canonical participant artifact.

the Library of Yourself™ is the participant-facing collection that houses Recognition Records™.

The terms are complementary and non-interchangeable.

“Part of” was chosen over “Stored in” because it communicates belonging rather than location.

> Recognition Record™  
> Part of the Library of Yourself™

The Declaration™ PDF evolved from a static document into a personalized Recognition Record™.

The engine now preserves participant wording, creates as many pages as needed, prevents orphaned headings, and keeps prompts with the opening of answers.

Current status: Verified Local. Production verification remains outstanding.
