## Movement One and Movement Two Copy Reopened; No-Forced-Answer Ruling

**Status:** History record. Preserves provenance. Does not supersede current constitutional authority.

## What happened

During a local Founder walkthrough of Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™, Diana reopened the previously Founder-locked participant-facing copy for the affected Movement One prompts, witnesses, Recognition Mirror™, Bedrock, Recognition Lens, closing, and the Movement One → Movement Two transition, plus the Movement Two opening question, because the prior wording felt mechanical, constructed, and leading rather than human and emotionally real.

The new wording implemented in its place is WORKING / FOUNDER REVIEW copy, pending another Founder walkthrough. It is not locked. The full old-to-new mapping is recorded in `docs/design-specifications/pathway-two-remember-copy-correction-2026-09-05.md`, which also updates the Movement One and Movement Two design specifications additively (without erasing the previously locked or working copy, which remains preserved in those documents as provenance).

## Founder ruling: no forced answer

Diana ruled that a participant must never be required to produce non-blank response text merely to continue through a writing screen, or to be treated as having completed a movement. A blank, absent, or incomplete response must never be fabricated, summarized, classified, or treated as evidence, in either direction. Movement completion (both Movement One and Movement Two) now relies solely on durable screen/view progression — reaching the movement's completion-trigger screen, which is only reachable by having advanced through every earlier screen in order, plus the movement's required viewed checkpoints. It no longer requires any response to contain non-whitespace text. Whole-pathway completion remains unaffected and unchanged.

## Reaffirmed governing principle

the codeXverse™ creates conditions for recognition. It must not lead, diagnose, interpret, steer, or construct a predetermined insight for the participant. No AI speech patterning or line/ladder stacking is permitted in participant-facing copy. These are not new rules; they restate `docs/canon/terminology.md`, `docs/standards/writing-standards.md`, and `docs/standards/participant-language.md`, applied here to a specific local implementation correction.
