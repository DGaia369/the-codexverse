## 4. Movement Excavation Method

Every movement must be excavated internally against the following private design process:

**Interrogate** → expose assumptions inside the movement title, purpose, and first answers. Required result: **Assumption Register**.

**Excavate** → go beneath behaviour into adaptation, protection, cost, fear, and belonging. Required result: **Hidden Mechanism**.

**DD** → remove explanations that make the pattern sound noble, harmless, inevitable, or complete. Required result: **Irreducible Recognition**.

**WEAPONIZE** → turn recognition into a lens capable of guiding future judgment without turning it into a vow, boundary, instruction, or Agreement™. Required result: **Recognition Lens**.

**Bedrock** → test whether anything meaningful can still move the conclusion. Required result: **Bedrock Question, Deepest Answer, and Closing Recognition**.

**DD is not cruelty. It is where compassion is not permitted to become camouflage.**

**WEAPONIZE does not mean telling the participant what to do.** Inside Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™, it means she acquires recognition language and discernment she can use later. Authored decisions, promises, Agreements™, and enacted boundaries belong beyond this pathway.

These excavation stages are **not participant-facing labels** and must not become a visible self-help ladder.

## 5. Six-Movement Excavation Direction

### Movement One: See the Scattering
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> I became skilled at disappearing in ways that could look like love, competence, loyalty, patience, or peace.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> When the room changes, where does my attention go, and who do I become?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> What did becoming necessary protect you from having to find out about whether you could belong without the role?

### Movement Two: Separate the Self from the Role
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> Some of what I call “me” is mine. Some is what I learned to keep performing so I could remain recognizable, useful, or safe.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> Would I still choose this if my belonging were not at stake?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> If no one needed the role tomorrow, what would still be true about you?

### Movement Three: Recover What Was Suppressed
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> What I learned to call “too much” may have carried information the arrangement could not afford to hear.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> What information becomes available if I stop treating this part of me as the problem?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> What did you have to make wrong in yourself so the arrangement could remain right?

### Movement Four: Remain With Herself
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> Self-abandonment often happens in the space between knowing and explaining what I know away.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> What did I know before I began arguing against myself?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> Can you remain with what is true before deciding what it requires?

### Movement Five: Recognize Access
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> What I called closeness may sometimes have included access I never consciously learned to see.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> Is this connection, or is this access I stopped noticing?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> What has been treated as available simply because it has always been available?

### Movement Six: Experience Internal Reunion
**Irreducible Recognition:**  
> I was not divided because my parts were incompatible. I was divided by rules about which parts were allowed to count.

**Recognition Lens:**  
> Which part of me is being asked to disappear so another part can remain acceptable?

**Bedrock territory:**  
> What changes when no part of you has to leave for the rest of you to belong?

## 6. Emerging Pathway Mechanism: Self-Editing in Service of Belonging

A deeper mechanism has now become visible.

Self-abandonment is not always dramatic. It may occur through subtle edits: a slight withholding, a softened opinion, dimming a strength or ambition, waiting to learn what everyone else wants before naming what she wants, forgiving before she has processed, making herself easier to remain connected to, or selectively omitting parts of herself that might disturb the arrangement.

Internal working term: **Self-editing in service of belonging.**

The performance may have worked. It may have reduced friction, preserved access, prevented conflict, made her useful, or allowed her to remain part of the picture.

The deeper DD recognition is that the strategy could secure a place for the representative while making it increasingly difficult for the real woman to know whether she herself was being received.

This mechanism applies even when the participant is happily married or otherwise in a loving relationship. Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™ does not require a villain. The room may be harmful, loving, limited, or simply operating alongside adaptation the participant brought with her.

The pathway is excavating the mechanism of self-editing, not prosecuting another person.

## 10. Randomization Requirement

Randomization is a governing participant-experience requirement modeled on Pathway One™.

**Stable:** Governing prompts and recognition sequence remain stable so the recognition mechanism does not fracture.

**May be randomized:** Curated pools may be used for arrival language, optional role cues, helper language, witness fragments, pause language, and transition fragments.

Role cues are invitations, not assignments. The participant may ignore every cue and write her own language.

### Sticky randomization
Once a participant/session receives a randomized element, the assignment must persist.

It must **not reroll** on refresh, browser close/reopen, device change, logout/login, or later resume.

The same participant should return to the same assigned experience.
