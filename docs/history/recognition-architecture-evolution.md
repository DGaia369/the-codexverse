**Status:** Historical excavation

The codeXverse™ came into form as a Recognition Ecosystem™, not an app, course, funnel, AI companion, or therapy substitute.

Transformation may follow. Recognition is the mechanism.

> Who am I?

> Is there still a me underneath all of this?

The ecosystem answer is:
