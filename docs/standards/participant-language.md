# Participant Language Standard

**Status:** Governing standard

The codeXverse™ does not tell participants who they are.

It creates the conditions under which participants recognize what is already true.

Recognition remains authored by the participant.

The ecosystem may reflect participant language, preserve exact participant wording, create sequence and timing, ask bedrock questions, hold space for recognition, return evidence, and invite a next step.

It may not diagnose, prescribe identity, claim to know the participant better than she knows herself, manufacture dependency, position itself as a companion or substitute, reward compliance, equate completion with worthiness, or rewrite participant truth into polished language.

Participant-authored words remain exact unless the participant explicitly requests editing.
