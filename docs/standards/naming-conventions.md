# Naming Conventions

**Status:** Governing standard

Preserve the lowercase opening article even at the beginning of a sentence.

Correct:

- the codeXverse™
- the Library of Yourself™
- the Telling™
- the Cracking™
- the Agreement™
- the Declaration™
- the Noticing™

Do not auto-capitalize the opening article.

Preserve ™ where the canonical term includes it.

Recognition Record™ is the canonical artifact.

the Library of Yourself™ is the participant-facing collection that houses Recognition Records™.

Use:

> Recognition Record™  
> Part of the Library of Yourself™

Do not use one term as a replacement for the other.
