# Writing Standards

**Status:** Governing standard

Participant-facing language must create the conditions for recognition. It must not tell the participant who she is, explain her to herself, or perform insight on her behalf.

## Prohibited AI speech patterns

Do not use ladder stacking, repetitive one-line cadence, parallel sentence piles, fake profundity, motivational filler, unnecessary adjectives, inflated emotional language, explanations of what the participant already knows, performative certainty, or artificial intimacy.

## Punctuation

No em dashes in participant-facing copy.

Use commas, periods, colons, or natural sentence restructuring.

Avoid excessive ellipses and fragment piles used only for dramatic effect.

## Rhythm

Write in grounded, human paragraphs.

Use short lines only when the interface or ritual genuinely requires them.

## Voice

Clear, warm, precise, sovereign, and unforced.

Recognition before explanation.

Truth before performance.

Invitation without pressure.
