## Explicit Exclusions — Restated, Not Relaxed

This document does not touch:

- The naming, definition, function, content, form, and creation point of the **Second Inheritance™** remain intentionally undefined. Only its position as the inheritance associated with Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™ is established. Nothing in this Design Specification assigns it a purpose, determines what it gathers or contains, defines what form it takes, places it within a specific movement, or defines its relationship to the Library of Yourself™.
- **Pathway Three™** or the Agreement™. ReMEMBER™'s Completion Recognition prepares the ground for Pathway Three™ but does not build, name, or design any part of it.
- **Payment and subscription architecture.** ReMEMBER™ is the first paid pathway per existing canon, but no billing, tiering, or access-control implementation is designed here.

Where a design choice below could foreclose one of these later, it is flagged inline as a **forward compatibility note**.

## Governing Constraints Inherited From Canon

- **Nervous System Architecture:** Safety, then truth, then invitation, in that order, in every screen and every transition.
- **Language Frequency Architecture:** Tier 2 runs on Return as primary frequency, Recognition continuing. Truth, Sovereignty, and Integration belong to later tiers except where a specific movement's design calls for a narrow, flagged exception.
- **Recognition Laws™:** particularly Law 1 (the deepest guidance is recognized, not given) and Law 3 (humans only change what they can safely see).
- **The Surrender Principle™:** no movement asks for surrender. Each movement creates conditions; surrender, if it occurs, occurs in the participant.
- **Voice Standard:** Phoenix Voice. No em dashes. No AI speech patterning. No Tier language participant-facing.
- **Build Discipline:** nothing here is canon until Diana approves it, movement by movement, at the point of implementation.

## Part One: The Participant Journey — Overview

ReMEMBER™ is the second recognition in the three-recognition architectural sequence:

> Pathway One™ — *There you are.*
> Pathway Two™: ReMEMBER™ — *All of you belongs here.*
> Pathway Three™ — *This is what I will no longer negotiate away.*

The participant entering ReMEMBER™ has already completed Pathway One™. She knows a self exists beneath the adaptation. She does not yet consistently live as that self. She still organizes her life around roles, still settles for crumbs, still allows other voices to overrule her own.

ReMEMBER™ does not ask her to become someone new. It asks her to gather back what became scattered across roles, relationships, responsibilities, and survival adaptations, without turning wholeness into isolation, hardness, perfection, or another standard she must earn.

The pathway is not memory. It is reassembly.

### The Six Movements

1. **See the Scattering** — she recognizes she did not lose herself; she distributed herself.
2. **Separate the Self from the Role** — she distinguishes who she is from who she became to remain connected.
3. **Recover What Was Suppressed** — she begins calling back what the roles required her to silence.
4. **Remain With Herself** — she practices staying present with herself under pressure to leave.
5. **Recognize Access** — she sees the relationship between access and self-abandonment.
6. **Experience Internal Reunion** — the scattered parts are no longer strangers to one another.

### The Three-Part Grouping

| Section | Movements | Function |
|---|---|---|
| The Scattering | One | See where she became distributed, identify one lived role |
| The Returning | Two, Three, Four | Separate self from role, recover the suppressed, practice remaining |
| The ReMEMBERing | Five, Six | Recognize access, experience internal reunion |

### The Completion Condition

ReMEMBER™ does not end with a promise. It ends with a recognition.

Not: *I will never leave myself again.*

But: *I am here with myself.*

The first belongs to Pathway Three™, where it becomes an authored Agreement™. ReMEMBER™ produces the wholeness the Agreement™ will later protect. It does not produce the Agreement™ itself.

## Part Two: Entry Threshold — LOCKED

**Governing frequency:** Recognition, continuing from Pathway One™. No Truth frequency yet.

**Function:** acknowledge she has already crossed once. Do not re-ask what Pathway One™ already answered.

**Screen 2**
> Now something else is asking to be seen.

## Part Three: Movement One — See the Scattering — LOCKED

**Governing frequency:** Recognition.

**Function:** the first interaction should help her identify one lived role, not every role in her life.

**Prompt 1**
> Where do you find yourself becoming responsible for everyone else's comfort?

**Prompt 2**
> Who do you become in that room, relationship, or role?

**Prompt 3**
> What does that version of you know how to do exceptionally well?

**Prompt 4**
> What does she keep hidden so she can keep doing it?

**Prompt 5**
> What part of you goes quiet when she takes over?

**Prompt 6**
> What makes this version of you feel necessary in that room?

**Closing Recognition**
> The role was real.
> It was not all of you.

## Part Four: Movement Two — Separate the Self from the Role
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing frequency:** Recognition primary, Return beginning to surface.

**Function:** the participant begins distinguishing between who she is, who she became to remain connected, what she learned to carry, what she was expected to suppress, and what once protected her but now keeps her divided. The role is not treated as an enemy.

> What protected me is not automatically who I am.

**Structural design:**

This movement should return to the specific role Movement One surfaced, rather than introducing a new one.

Proposed screen sequence (functional, not final):

1. Re-present her own Movement One answers to Prompt 1 and Prompt 2 back to her, unedited.
2. A question surfacing what the role protected her from, rather than what it accomplished.
3. A question distinguishing the role's intelligence from the role's cost.
4. A closing recognition naming the separation, without asking her to reject the role.

**Design considerations:**

- This is the first movement where the participant's own prior answers are reflected back to her. The mechanism for this should be scoped as a reusable pattern, since later movements likely need it again.

## Part Five: Movement Three — Recover What Was Suppressed
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing frequency:** Recognition continuing, Return primary.

**Function:** she begins calling back parts the roles required her to silence or postpone. Not a list of qualities she should develop. She recognizes which parts are already hers.

> This did not disappear.
> I stopped allowing it to belong.

**Structural design:**

Proposed screen sequence (functional, not final):

1. An open question letting her name what is missing in her own words first, before any list is offered as a gentle aid if she is stuck.
2. A question locating where, specifically, this suppression happens, tied back to the role named in Movements One and Two.
3. A closing recognition using her own language for what she named.

**Design considerations:**

- **Content sensitivity flag:** the source list includes her sexuality. Any prompt or reflection touching this must stay at the level of recognition and language the participant herself chooses, never system-suggested specifics.
- This movement is emotionally heavier than Movement One or Two. Safety must be re-established at the start of this movement even within a continuous or resumed session.

## Part Six: Movement Four — Remain With Herself
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing frequency:** Return primary, first careful use of Truth frequency.

**Function:** the central practice of ReMEMBER™: presence with herself. She learns to hear herself before replacing her own voice with someone else's preference.

> I do not leave myself.

The practice is not winning the confrontation. It is remaining present with what she knows. Wholeness does not require the feelings to disappear. It requires that she does not disappear because they arrived.

**Structural design:**

Proposed screen sequence (functional, not final):

1. A question asking her to locate one real, recent moment where she felt the pull to disappear rather than stay honest.
2. A question distinguishing the feeling that arose from the action she took in response.
3. A question naming what staying, instead, might have looked like, as her own imagined recognition, not instruction.
4. A closing recognition affirming that the feelings do not have to leave for her to stay.

**Design considerations:**

- This movement must not become instructional. Per Recognition Law 1, it helps her recognize a moment she already lived, not tells her how to remain present.
- This is the natural home for the JB Principle. Not scoped for build now; flagged as a future design pass on adaptive pacing.

## Part Seven: Movement Five — Recognize Access
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing frequency:** Return continuing, Sovereignty beginning to surface carefully. This is a deliberate, narrow exception ahead of Sovereignty's normal Tier 4 home, and not a precedent for pulling that frequency earlier elsewhere in Tier 2.

**Function:** she begins recognizing that care, history, need, proximity, obligation, guilt, family, affection, or familiarity do not automatically give another person unrestricted access to her. She does not yet author the Agreement™.

**Structural design:**

Proposed screen sequence (functional, not final):

1. A question surfacing one relationship or context where access has required her silence or shrinking.
2. A question distinguishing closeness from access.
3. A closing recognition naming that discernment is not rejection.

**Design considerations:**

- Highest risk of drifting toward Pathway Three™ territory. Any screen copy that starts to sound like a boundary being set, rather than a pattern being seen, does not belong here.
- **Forward compatibility note:** this movement is the most likely candidate to be referenced when Pathway Three™ is eventually designed. Nothing about its data model needs to anticipate that connection structurally now.

---

## Part Eight: Movement Six — Experience Internal Reunion
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing frequency:** Integration, arriving for the first time in ReMEMBER™.

**Function:** the participant completes ReMEMBER™ when the scattered parts of her are no longer strangers to one another. The outcome is not invulnerability. It is internal relationship.

**Structural design:**

This movement functions as a gathering, not a new excavation.

Proposed screen sequence (functional, not final):

1. A reflective return to what she named across Movements One through Five, her own language, re-presented.
2. A question asking what she notices when she sees all of it together, rather than asking her to summarize or perform insight.
3. A closing recognition, likely the pathway's central line, arrived at through her own gathered language rather than delivered as a system statement.

## Part Nine: Completion Recognition
### Proposed Architecture — No Copy Approved

**Governing principle, per canon:**

> The pathway is complete when she can remain connected to herself without requiring another person's permission.
> She belongs to herself.

**What Completion Recognition is not:** a vow, a boundary statement, a progress summary, or a badge.

**What Completion Recognition is:** a single moment where what has been recognized across all six movements settles, without being turned into a promise about the future.

**Proposed direction (functional, not final):**

> I am here with myself.

Explicitly not: *I will never leave myself again.* That belongs to Pathway Three™.

**Design considerations:**

- Completion Recognition should mark readiness for Pathway Three™ internally, comparably to how `declarations.status = 'sealed'` marks Pathway One™ completion for the `/record` gate already built. This document does not specify the mechanism, only that whatever marks ReMEMBER™ complete should be queryable the same clean way existing evidence already is.

## Part Ten: Progression Architecture

**Movement Sequencing:** sequential within a session, one screen at a time.

**Resume Behavior:** the approved Movement One direction (session-and-response model, one response per prompt, resumable) is the pattern for all six movements. A pathway-level session record (which movement she is in, whether each is complete) sits naturally above the per-response model, the same relationship `declarations` already has to its fifteen fields. Whether that is a new table or an extension is an implementation decision for whoever builds Movement Two onward, not decided here.

**Non-Linearity:** per the Recognition Loop™, revisiting is deepening, not repeating. This document scopes the first pass only.

**Internal Completion Tracking:** exists for gating and continuity only, never rendered as a progress bar, percentage, or checklist, mirroring the existing design law already applied to `/record`.
