## 2. Governing principle restated

the codeXverse™ creates conditions for recognition. It must not lead, diagnose, interpret, steer, or construct a predetermined insight for the participant. Participant-facing language must feel human and emotionally real.

This session's correction specifically targeted:
- AI speech patterning
- ladder/line stacking
- mechanical or clinical phrasing
- interpretive labels
- assumed fear, assumed belonging concerns, assumed self-abandonment, assumed suppression
- helper copy that explains what the participant is supposed to discover

The participant's own words remain the evidence. Nothing below classifies, diagnoses, or tells the participant which parts are "really her."

Canonical naming rule, reaffirmed: always write **the codeXverse™**, article "the" lowercase, including at the start of a sentence.

## 4. No-forced-answer rule — Founder ruling

A participant must never be forced to manufacture an answer merely to continue.

A participant may have no answer yet, an empty response, "I don't know," or something incomplete, without being treated as failing the movement.

Implementation:
- The client-side validation message "This deserves an answer before you continue." was removed. A blank writing screen now advances on Continue exactly like a filled one.
- A blank or absent response is never saved as fabricated text, summarized, classified, or treated as evidence, in either direction.
- Movement completion (`utils/remember.ts`, `tryCompleteMovement`) no longer checks whether any response contains non-whitespace text. It now checks only that the movement's required checkpoints (Recognition Mirror™ viewed, Recognition Lens viewed) have been durably recorded, together with having reached the movement's completion-trigger screen — which itself is only reachable by having advanced through every earlier screen in the Founder-locked sequence, one exact step at a time (`getNextAllowedScreenKey`). This is the durable screen/view progression referred to in the Founder's ruling, replacing non-blank response text as proof of participation.
- This applies to both Movement One and Movement Two.
